🎯 4D Number Lab

Explore historical four-digit results and generate random number ideas. This is an educational tool, not a reliable prediction system. In a fair draw, every four-digit number has the same chance.

1. Analyze historical results

Paste one four-digit result per line. Commas, semicolons, or spaces are also accepted. Leading zeros are preserved.

2. Generate random 4D numbers

Numbers are sampled uniformly without duplicates within each batch. They are random ideas, not forecasts.

3. How to use the analysis

The digit counts summarize only the results you enter. Historical frequencies do not make a digit or combination more likely in a future independent draw. This page processes everything locally in your browser; it does not upload or save your entries.
